Travel

Getting to Ithaca and Cornell University

By Air

Ithaca Tompkins International Airport (ITH) is the closest airport, located about 15 minutes from campus. It has direct flights from several hubs including Newark (EWR), Philadelphia (PHL), and Detroit (DTW).

For more flight options, consider flying into Syracuse Hancock International Airport (SYR), approximately 1 hour north, or Binghamton (BGM), approximately 1 hour south. Car rentals are available at all airports.

By Car

Ithaca is accessible via several major highways.

From New York City

~4.5 hours via I-81 North or Route 17 West. Scenic route through the Catskills also available.

From Philadelphia

~4 hours via I-81 North through Scranton and Binghamton.

From Boston

~5 hours via I-90 West (Massachusetts Turnpike) through Albany and Syracuse.

From Toronto

~4 hours via QEW to I-90 through Buffalo and the Finger Lakes. Valid passport required for border crossing.

Visitor parking is available on Cornell's campus. A parking permit or daily pass may be required — details will be provided closer to the meeting.

By Bus

Several intercity bus services connect Ithaca to major cities. FlixBus and OurBus operate routes from New York City. Buses arrive at the Ithaca bus terminal downtown, from which TCAT buses connect to campus.

Local Transportation

TCAT (Tompkins Consolidated Area Transit) provides regular bus service between downtown Ithaca, Cornell campus, and surrounding areas. Rideshare services (Uber, Lyft) are also available in Ithaca, though availability can be limited compared to larger cities. Campus itself is walkable, though hilly.

International travelers: Please check visa requirements well in advance. If you need an invitation letter for your visa application, contact the organizers and we will be happy to provide one.
