Code of Conduct

Creating a welcoming and respectful environment

The Phytochemical Society of North America is committed to providing a safe, productive, and welcoming environment for all meeting participants, regardless of race, ethnicity, national origin, age, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, religion, or career stage.

Expected Behavior

All participants, speakers, exhibitors, organizers, and volunteers at PSNA 2027 are expected to:

Treat all individuals with respect and consideration. Communicate openly and thoughtfully with fellow attendees. Be considerate of different viewpoints and experiences. Give proper credit to the contributions and intellectual property of others. Refrain from photography or recording of presentations unless given explicit permission by the presenter. Alert conference organizers if you notice a situation that may violate this code of conduct.

Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment and discrimination in any form will not be tolerated. This includes offensive verbal comments, deliberate intimidation, stalking, harassing photography or recording, sustained disruption of talks or events, unwelcome physical contact, and unwelcome sexual attention.

Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to any member of the organizing committee. Reports can be made in person during the meeting or by email to the conference organizers (see Contact page). All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.

Consequences

Participants asked to stop any harassing or discriminatory behavior are expected to comply immediately. Conference organizers may take action including warning the offender, expulsion from the conference without refund, or reporting to the participant's institution, as appropriate to the severity of the violation.

This Code of Conduct applies to all PSNA 2027 events and spaces, including sessions, poster halls, workshops, social events, and online communications related to the meeting.
