Conference Location

The PSNA 2027 meeting will be held on the campus of Cornell University in Ithaca, New York. Cornell sits high above Cayuga Lake in the Finger Lakes region, surrounded by gorges, waterfalls, and some of the most scenic landscapes in the northeastern United States.

About Ithaca & the Finger Lakes

Ithaca is a vibrant college town known for its natural beauty, farm-to-table dining, local wineries, and outdoor recreation. The surrounding Finger Lakes region offers excellent hiking (especially the famous gorge trails), wine tasting, and lake activities. Late June is a beautiful time to visit, with warm weather and long days.
