Welcome

Join us for the 66th Annual Meeting of The Phytochemical Society of North America (PSNA) at Cornell University in beautiful Ithaca, New York. The meeting will feature cutting-edge research spanning the chemistry, biochemistry, synthetic biology, evolution, and chemical ecology of plant natural products, as well as their impact on plant and human physiology.

PSNA is a highly supportive community of scientists dedicated to advancing phytochemical research. We emphasize supporting early-career researchers by selecting many oral presentations from submitted abstracts and offering numerous awards for students, postdocs, and beginning investigators.

We look forward to welcoming you to Cornell!
