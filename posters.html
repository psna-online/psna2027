Posters & Awards

Poster presentations and travel awards

Poster Presentations

Poster sessions are a central part of the PSNA meeting, providing an interactive format for researchers to share their work. PSNA particularly encourages submissions from graduate students, postdocs, and early-career investigators.

Poster Guidelines

Poster dimensions: Maximum size 48" wide × 36" tall (landscape orientation preferred). Poster boards and pushpins will be provided. Detailed guidelines will be sent to presenters after abstract acceptance.

Abstract Submission

Submit your abstract for a poster presentation using the form link below. Abstracts should be no more than 250 words and include the title, authors, affiliations, and a brief description of the research.

Poster abstract deadline: May 25, 2027

Travel Awards

PSNA offers travel awards to support students and postdocs attending the annual meeting. Awards help offset the costs of registration, travel, and lodging.

Student Travel Awards

Open to graduate students presenting a poster or oral presentation. Must be a PSNA member. Application requires an abstract and a brief statement of need.

Deadline: April 1, 2027

Postdoc Travel Awards

Available for postdoctoral researchers within 3 years of completing their PhD. PSNA membership required.

Deadline: April 1, 2027

For more information on PSNA awards, visit the PSNA Awards page.

Poster Award Prizes

Outstanding poster presentations by students and postdocs will be recognized with poster awards at the conference banquet. All poster presenters who are students or postdocs are automatically considered.
