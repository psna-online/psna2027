Lodging

Accommodation options near Cornell

Conference hotel block details coming soon. We are negotiating group rates with hotels near campus. Details and booking links will be posted here once confirmed. We recommend booking early — Ithaca hotels fill up quickly in summer.

On-Campus Housing

Cornell University may offer dormitory-style housing for conference attendees at reduced rates. This is a budget-friendly option within walking distance of the venue. Details and availability will be confirmed.

Nearby Hotels

The following hotels are conveniently located near campus. Note that these are suggestions and do not imply endorsement — negotiate rates are being arranged separately.

The Statler Hotel

On-campus at the Cornell School of Hotel Administration. Steps from most campus buildings. A convenient choice for attendees.

statlerhotel.cornell.edu

Hilton Garden Inn Ithaca

Located in downtown Ithaca on the Ithaca Commons. About 10 minutes from campus by car or bus.

Best Western University Inn

On East Hill near campus. Shuttle service may be available during the conference.

Country Inn & Suites

Located off Route 13, approximately 10 minutes from campus. Free parking and breakfast included.

La Tourelle Hotel

Boutique hotel near Buttermilk Falls State Park, about 10 minutes from campus. Scenic setting.

Budget Options

Additional budget-friendly options are available in downtown Ithaca and along Route 13. Airbnb and VRBO rentals are also plentiful in the area.

Getting Around

TCAT (Tompkins Consolidated Area Transit) provides public bus service throughout Ithaca and Cornell campus. Many routes connect downtown hotels directly to campus. Cornell also offers free campus bus service.
